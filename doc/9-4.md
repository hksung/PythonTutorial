## 1. Review the inspection results

Review the Inspection activities from [L2SCA](9-2.html) and [LxGrTgr](9-3.html). Use your notes and the output files to answer the following questions.

1. Which L2SCA index did you inspect? Define it and state whether your manual calculation matched the automatic result.

2. Which LxGrTgr tag did you inspect? Define the structure it represents and identify any missing or unexpected tags.

3. Compare the two tools. What does each tool measure, and what kind of output does each produce?

4. Based on your inspection, what are the strengths and limitations of automated complexity analysis? Give at least one example from each tool.

5. Write a short summary that explains why researchers should inspect automated annotations before using the results in an analysis.

## 2. Explore proficiency and complexity

Analyze the texts in `sample_proficiency` with L2SCA and/or LxGrTgr. Choose one or two indices or tags that you expect to be related to writing proficiency.

1. State your prediction. For example, do you expect higher-proficiency writers to use more complex nominals per clause, longer T-units, or more instances of a particular LxGrTgr tag? Explain why.

2. Compare texts at different proficiency levels.

3. Describe the relationship. Does the selected feature appear more often, less often, or in a different form across proficiency levels?

4. Evaluate the evidence. Explain whether your observations support your prediction. Consider alternative explanations such as text length, genre, topic, or individual writing style.
